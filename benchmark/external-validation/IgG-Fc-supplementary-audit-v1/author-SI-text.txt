Figure S2. Previously reported X-ray co-crystal structure of the IgG-binding peptide and human IgG1 (PDB: 
6IQH) visualized using UCSF Chimera software. The IgG-binding peptide and antibody are shown as cream 
and pink ribbons, respectively, highlighting the regions around the Asp3 and Thr15 residues on the peptide.

structure and (d) Thr15 substitution. CD spectra of peptides including (e) Lys8Leu substitution and (f) IAPG-
1 derivatives. The peptides Lys8Leu/Tyr6(1Nal) and Lys8Leu/Tyr6(2Nal) cannot measure the spectra because 
of poor solubility to assay conditions.
